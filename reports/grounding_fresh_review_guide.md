## fresh-draft-007

**Question:** Under section 4 of the Industrial Relations Code, what is the maximum size of a grievance redressal committee and what representation requirements apply?

**Expected:** No more than ten members; equal employer and worker representation, and adequate women-worker representation not less than their proportion in the establishment.

**Source:** The Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (No. 35 of 2020), PDF page 8, section 4(2), 4(4) and proviso.

(3) The chairperson of the Grievance Redressal Committee shall be selected from among persons representing the employer and the workers alternatively on rotational basis every year. (4) The total number of members of the Grievance Redressal Committee shall not exceed ten:
