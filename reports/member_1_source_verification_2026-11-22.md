## Redistribution decision

The team release uses the registry and official links. Bulk PDF, page-image, and corpus/OCR redistribution is outside that release. Section 52(1)(q) of the Copyright Act, 1957 distinguishes Official Gazette matter from Acts of a legislature and attaches conditions to reproducing Acts. This does not settle permission for a mixed corpus containing FAQs, handbooks, letters, illustrations, or third-party content. No blanket clearance was established. Any later proposal to redistribute should identify the specific files, the basis and conditions for each, and any needed permissions.
